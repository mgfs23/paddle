Paddle - midi chord generator firmware for M5stack Cardputer ADV

Plug in your Cardputer via USB and click the button. Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

Paddle: Quick Guide

What it is

Paddle is a pocket chord instrument for the M5Stack Cardputer. You choose a scale and play chords by pressing two keys. It has a built-in synthesizer and can also control an external one over BLE MIDI or a UART (DIN) MIDI cable. It includes an arpeggiator and a tilt-based gesture controller that uses the built-in motion sensor.

Playing chords

  • Degree keys q w e r t y u i o p [ ] select a scale degree. The first key is the root, and the next keys follow the chosen scale.
  • Type keys l k j h g f d s a m n b v c x z select a chord type. The available types are shown on screen under the chord name.
  • Play: hold a degree key and press a type key. Pressing another type key changes the chord.
  • Enter stops the sound and sends All Notes Off.
  • Tab cycles the MIDI output (BLE → USB → UART).
  • - / = change the volume. Ctrl + - / = shift the octave by up to ±3.
  • Ctrl + 1…9 select a chord voicing: 3, 4 or 5 notes, each with root position and first or second inversion. Ctrl + 0 selects the standard voicing.
  • Esc goes back or leaves a menu.

In Settings use ; and . to move, , and / to change a value, and digits to type a number. You can set tempo source (internal, UART or BLE clock), BPM, MIDI in and out channels, output port, clock out over BLE, and the BLE device.

In the BLE device list use ; and . to move, / to connect or disconnect, , to rescan, and t to send a test note.

Arpeggiator

Turn it on with 3, then hold Ctrl and press:

  • q…] to set the note rate, from 1/2 to 1/32.
  • a s d f g h j k l to set the pattern: Up, Down, Up-Down and Down-Up over 1 or 2 octaves, or Random.
  • z to clear all modifiers.
  • x c v b n m to toggle the modifiers: Rests few, Rests many, Extra rare, Extra often, Oct jump, and Rnd gate. Rnd gate randomizes both the note length and its velocity.

Tilt control

  • Space turns tilt control on or off. The square on screen shows the tilt.
  • By default, tilting right controls Mod, forward controls Velocity, back controls Pitch bend, and left controls Filter.
  • In CC mode (4), each direction sends its own CC. In menus 5–8, type or step the CC number, and press Space to invert the direction (127→0 instead of 0→127).
  • The ; , . / keys lock the current value of a direction (marked in red).
  • Del records a tilt movement and loops it back. Ctrl + Del records a free-length loop.
