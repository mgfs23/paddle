Chords for Paddle

Paste text with chords. Each chord will be replaced by a pair of keys: the first is the root note, the second is the chord type.

Chords are replaced on lines that contain only chords, and in the [Am] form inside lyrics. The bass note in chords like C/G is ignored. Unknown chords are replaced with the closest similar one. If the text contains H, then B is treated as Bb (German notation).
